# NikyNapkins POS
Before making this POS, I was manually changing cells in my excel sheet which was incredibily tedious and at times inaccurate due to human error. As I am attending markets with more and more foot traffic, an efficient way to track my inventory was crucial. 

The solution was to make a single-file, offline-friendly point-of-sale tool for running inventory at art markets. Upload a CSV of what you're bringing, ring up sales as they happen, and export an updated CSV when the market ends.
